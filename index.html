The Legend

About Neymar

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior, born February 5, 1992, is a Brazilian professional footballer widely regarded as one of the best players of his generation. Renowned for his flair, dribbling, and creativity, he began his career at Santos FC, where he announced himself to the world by winning the Copa Libertadores in 2011.

He later moved to Barcelona, forming a historic attacking trio with Messi and Suárez, winning the Champions League, La Liga, and multiple domestic cups. In 2017, he transferred to Paris Saint-Germain for a world-record fee, leading the club to numerous Ligue 1 titles and a Champions League final. He then took on a new challenge at Al Hilal in Saudi Arabia, adding more silverware to his collection.

Now, in a fairytale turn of events, Neymar has returned to his boyhood club Santos in 2026 to write the final chapter of his legendary career. He continues to represent Brazil with pride, holding the record as the nation's all-time top scorer with 80 goals in 130 appearances.
